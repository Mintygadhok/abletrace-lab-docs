PART 1 — THE RULE.  ⚠ MINTY'S RULINGS.
⚠⚠ EDITED ONLY ON MINTY'S EXPRESS PERMISSION, SOUGHT EACH TIME.

EVERY UNIT FIGURE ON EVERY SCREEN COMES FROM ONE OF FOUR PLACES.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1 · ING-REQ — THE RECIPE REQUIREMENT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What an MO needs of an ingredient or an intermediate.

    quantity per batch × (MO shipping units ÷ shipping units per batch)

Work it out fresh every time.
⚠ NEVER use the stored `batches` number. That is this same sum,
  already rounded.

QUANTITY PER BATCH takes the basis of the thing being consumed —
  UNITS for an intermediate, Kg for an ingredient.
THE SCALING FACTOR IS ALWAYS SHIPPING UNITS OVER SHIPPING UNITS.
  Dimensionless. It never changes the basis.

WORKED EXAMPLE (Minty, S108): a batch needs 0.5 units of the
intermediate and makes 6 shipping units. The MO is for 7.
    7 ÷ 6 × 0.5 = 0.5833 units
▶ THE MO IS A SCALED-UP BATCH. SCALE THE REQUIREMENT BY THE SAME FACTOR.

LIVES IN   subrecipematerials.qty          ingredients, Kg per batch
           subrecipeformulation.ship_qty   intermediates, units per batch
⚠ subrecipeformulation ALSO holds `qty` in Kg. ING-REQ is ship_qty.
  Reading qty gives a right-looking number in the wrong basis.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2 · PK-CASCADE — THE PACKAGING FIGURES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
How many pouches, cartons, cases an MO needs.

    MO shipping units × the pack ratio at each level

Level 1 is the single unit and it carries the unit weight. THAT IS THE
ONE PLACE A UNIT WEIGHT IS HELD ANYWHERE IN THE SYSTEM. Every higher
level's weight follows from it through the same ratios.

⚠ batches plays NO part, therefore there is NO rounding variance.
  A fractional packaging figure is a defect, not rounding.

LIVES IN   fopackaging.quantity           how many of the level below
           fopackaging.pack_level         the order of the levels
           fopackaging.whd_flag           marks the shipping unit row
           fopackaging.wgt_kgs_per_unit   THE UNIT WEIGHT, Level 1 row

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3 · STOCK ON HAND — THE WAREHOUSE BALANCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What is in the warehouse right now. TWO BALANCES, KEPT SEPARATELY.

PRODUCTS AND INTERMEDIATES — formulations.inventory_units, in units.
  UP    an MO is completed and production received
        stock is returned from an MO it was released to
        a dispatch order is cancelled
  DOWN  allocated to a dispatch order
        written off through a miscellaneous release
        released into another product's recipe as an intermediate

MATERIALS — materials.inventory, in kilograms.
  UP    received from the vendor
        returned from an MO it was released to
  DOWN  released to an MO
        written off through a miscellaneous release

HOW STOCK MOVES OUT
  Stock on hand → DO            ⚠ STOCK MOVES
  DO → packing slip             no move
  Packing slip → shipped        no move
  Packing slip → DO             no move
  DO cancelled → stock on hand  ⚠ STOCK MOVES

  IT MOVES ONLY AT THE DO, IN BOTH DIRECTIONS. Once allocated, that
  stock is spoken for and a planner must not count it as available —
  even though it is still in the building.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4 · PRD-TO-DATE — PRODUCTION TO DATE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The completed quantity for an MO. mlomanagement.received_units.

Manufacturing may deliver into the warehouse several times against one
MO. Each delivery is entered as a received lot, IN SHIPPING UNITS. The
weight is derived from it. NEVER THE REVERSE.

The completed quantity is the sum of those received lots, in shipping
units (weight derived).

Received lots are INTERNAL TO THE MO and are never referenced outside
it. Wherever the app shows completed quantity, it is that sum.

ONE MO, ONE PRODUCT LOT. Only ever goes up.

LIVES IN   mlomanagement.received_units   the MO total
           receiveproducts.qty            one receipt
⚠ NOT INTERCHANGEABLE. A per-receipt row must show the receipt's own
  count. Putting the MO total on every receipt row is wrong.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5 · WHICH THINGS CARRY A UNIT COUNT   ⚠ MINTY'S RULING, S112
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MATERIALS      kilograms ONLY. No unit count, anywhere, on any screen.
FORMULATIONS   units AND kilograms — whether sold to a customer,
               consumed as an intermediate, or both.

THE DISCRIMINATOR IS THE THING, NOT THE BLOCK.
  "The same thing appearing as a material line in one recipe and a
  product elsewhere carries units BECAUSE IT IS A PRODUCT, not because
  of where it sits on a screen."

✓ IN CODE THE TEST IS: does the row carry a formula_id?
✓ THE SCHEMA ALREADY AGREES — the release backend branches on
  Rec_Lot_id (material) vs Rec_Product_id (product).
  IT IS THE SCREENS THAT DO NOT.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6 · UNITS ARE THE ANCHOR END TO END   ⚠ MINTY'S RULING, S112
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Every intermediate-product figure, anywhere in the app INCLUDING
TRACEABILITY, renders as:

    <units># (<Kg> <uom>)          J104's frozen format

⚠ THE REASON IS CONSISTENCY, NOT ARITHMETIC. A Kg-anchored reading of
  the release chain is defensible — units convert to Kg once at receipt
  and every later step is Kg minus Kg. Minty read it that way and ruled
  the OTHER way, knowing the cost. Every defect this campaign has caught
  was caught because one figure disagreed with another. A single format
  everywhere is what makes the next mismatch announce itself.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE TEST
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Point at any unit number on any screen and say which of the four it
came from.

If the answer is "we divided a weight", THAT IS THE BUG.

WEIGHT IS ALWAYS WORKED OUT FROM UNITS.
UNITS ARE NEVER WORKED OUT FROM WEIGHT.

DISPLAY   three decimal places.
          ⚠ ROUND FOR DISPLAY ONLY. Keep the full number in the
            calculation and in the database.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DISPATCH COLUMNS — NOT A FIFTH SOURCE, BUT UNIT-STORED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  dispatchorders.packing_units   what was AUTHORISED on the DO
  dispatchorders.qty_shipped     what ACTUALLY shipped
  packingslipdos.shipped_qty     units on a packing slip line
⚠ packing_units AND qty_shipped ARE NOT INTERCHANGEABLE. A DO can ship
  more or less than authorised. Minty's ruling S97.
⚠ dispatchorders.qty_to_ship IS Kg AND SITS BESIDE THEM. TRAPS 1.
